# 07 · Day-7 report

In the dashboard: Pilots → the client → **Copy day-7 report**. It reads:

> Habari {business} 🙏 Your 7-day pilot is done:
> • KES {spend} → {chats} WhatsApp chats (about KES {cpc} each)
> • {serious} serious buyers · {visits} site visits booked
> • Winning ad: "{winner}"
> If the ads stop, next week's enquiries stop. Shall we keep them running on the Enquiry Engine (KES 10,000/month, introductory), with fresh ads every week?

Before you send it:
1. Fill every day's numbers (Ads Manager → the campaign → results; chats and visits from their WhatsApp labels).
2. Be honest about a weak week: say what you changed and what you'd do next. A client who trusts the numbers stays.
3. Offer the next step that fits: Enquiry Engine (always), website (KES 5,000 + KES 4,000/month, 12 months) if they have no site, the December Diaspora pack (KES 12,000) for land.
4. After **Converted**, set the monthly price in the dashboard and move them to the weekly rhythm (new ads weekly, report weekly).

After **Declined**, leave them with the report and the offer to restart any time. No pressure.
