# 01 · Offer form

Send with the quick reply **Start the pilot**. The ads are only as good as the offer, so don't build until you have all six.

**Land developer**
1. Project name and the exact location (send a WhatsApp location pin).
2. Plot size(s), and the price: cash and instalment.
3. Deposit and number of months.
4. Title status. Be honest: we only write "READY TITLE" if it is ready.
5. Site-visit days and times, and the pickup point if you offer transport.
6. 5-10 photos or short videos (drone clips are gold), and OK to say: "Do an official land search, walk the beacons, pay only into the company account".

**Property agency**
1. The area(s) you want leads for.
2. 3-5 available units: area, bedrooms, rent or price.
3. Photos of each (the more rooms the better).
4. Viewing days and times.
5. Amenities you can truthfully claim (backup water, lift, secure parking...).
6. Who answers WhatsApp, and when.

If a client can't answer 1-4, **don't start the pilot**: it will waste their KES 5,000. Help them tidy the offer first.

Save the answers in the dashboard (Pilots → the client → Offer form). They go into `offer.json` for the final videos.
