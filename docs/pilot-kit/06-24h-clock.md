# 06 · From "yes" to live in 24 hours

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| Before the yes | The plan page already shows their 3 ads (sample offer re-dressed with their name, logo, area) |
| T+0-30 min | Send the 5 questions + offer form + M-Pesa request + the access guide for their route |
| T+30-90 min | Access granted, money in. Run `generate --final --offer` |
| T+2-3 h | Build the campaign (doc 05) + WhatsApp set-up call (doc 04) + submit |
| T+3-24 h | Meta review → live. First daily update the next morning |

Expect about 80-90% of pilots live inside 24 hours once **Raswal's ad account is warmed up** (a KES 1-2k ad for Raswal itself, a verified
Business Portfolio, a working card plus a backup). Two things you don't control: Meta's review time and how fast the client grants access.

Rules for running client money through your account:
- The budget is prepaid by M-Pesa before any spend.
- Keep a backup card and a second ad account ready, so one declined card or one restricted account doesn't stop every client.
- Meta adds Kenyan VAT: say "KES 5,000 all-in" (about KES 4,300 reaches the ads).
