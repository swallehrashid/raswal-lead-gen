# 04 · WhatsApp set-up call (20 minutes, on their phone)

You do not need access to their WhatsApp. Talk them through it.

1. **Greeting message** (Settings → Business tools → Greeting message). Use the greeting in `captions.txt` for their ad
   ("Karibu {business}! Thanks for reaching out. To help you quickly: 1) ... 2) ... 3) ...").
2. **Away message** for after 8pm: "Asante for your message. We reply from 8am."
3. **Quick replies** (type `/` to use): `/price` `/location` `/title` `/sitevisit` (agency: `/rent` `/area` `/viewing`). Fill in their real answers.
4. **Labels**: New lead → Visit booked → Paid.
5. **Catalogue** with their projects or listings (optional but nice).
6. Agree the rule: **reply within 15 minutes, 8am-8pm**. Slow replies are the biggest leak.

Test by messaging their number from yours and checking the greeting arrives.
