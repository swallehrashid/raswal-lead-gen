# 02 · Route checklist

Ask these five at "yes" (they take two minutes on WhatsApp):

1. Do you have a Facebook Page for the business? Send me the link.
2. Is your WhatsApp the **Business** app? Which number should get the enquiries?
3. Have you run Facebook ads before, and does your card still work on that ad account?
4. Is the Page admin available today?
5. The offer form (doc 01).

| Answers | Route | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Already advertises, card works | **A** | They add Raswal as a partner on their Page **and** ad account ("Manage campaigns"). They pay Meta directly. |
| Has a Page, never advertised | **B** (most common) | They give Raswal ads access on the Page and link their WhatsApp to it. They send KES 5,000 by M-Pesa. We run it from Raswal's ad account. |
| No Page | **C** | Create the Page together on a 10-minute call, **on their phone** so it is theirs. Then Route B. |
| WhatsApp can't be linked | **Backup** | The ad button goes to their `wa.me` link. Still opens their WhatsApp chat. |
| Won't give any access | avoid | Meta is starting to let the WhatsApp Business app make ads without Facebook. Not confirmed for Kenya, and you lose control. |

**Housing category:** if Ads Manager asks for it, accept it. Targeting becomes location-only and every ad already works that way.

**Money:** the pilot budget is always **prepaid by M-Pesa before** you spend it. Never front a client's budget.
